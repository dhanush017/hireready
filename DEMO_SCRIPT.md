# HireReady AI — 3-Minute Video Demo Script ⏱️
> Track: Ship It · WeMakeDevs "First Commit" Hackathon  
> Target Duration: Exactly 3 Minutes (180 seconds)

---

### [0:00 – 0:20] The Problem (Hook)
- **Visual**: Screen recording showing open LinkedIn / Internshala rejection emails, then cut to HireReady AI landing page.
- **Voiceover**:  
  *"Every semester, engineering students send out hundreds of internship applications into a black hole. Traditional ATS checkers give arbitrary percentage scores with zero explanation or actionable next steps. Students are left wondering: What skills am I actually missing? Why do they matter? And what should I do next? Meet HireReady AI — your placement copilot that turns resume gaps into career roadmaps."*

---

### [0:20 – 0:40] Load Demo & Input Flexibility
- **Visual**: Move cursor over the hero header, click `"Load Demo (B.Tech CSE vs SWE Intern)"`. Show resume text and internship JD populate instantly.
- **Voiceover**:  
  *"Students can either upload a PDF resume under 2 MB or paste their plain text, paired with any target job description. For this demo, let's load Aarav Sharma, a 3rd-year B.Tech CSE student with C++, Python, and SQL skills, applying for a Software Engineer Intern role at CloudScale Tech."*

---

### [0:40 – 1:10] Real-Time Analysis & Transparent Score
- **Visual**: Click `"Analyze Match & Gap Breakdown"`. Point out the 4 loading steps advancing in real time. Arrive at Results Screen.
- **Voiceover**:  
  *"Notice our real-time pipeline: parallel Strands Agents parse the resume and job requirements using Amazon Nova Lite on Amazon Bedrock. Rather than guessing an arbitrary score, our deterministic Python scoring engine applies weighted matching. Aarav scores a 71% match. We provide a full mathematical breakdown and an explicit disclaimer: this is an evidence-based assessment, not an opaque hiring prediction."*

---

### [1:10 – 1:35] Evidence-Based Skill Gaps
- **Visual**: Switch to `"Skill Gap Cards"` tab. Hover over AWS and Docker cards.
- **Voiceover**:  
  *"Look at the skill gap breakdown. For AWS, our related-skills map spotted Aarav's SQLite and backend work for partial credit, but highlights missing cloud experience. For Docker, it flags missing containerization as a high priority for this internship, citing explicit evidence directly from the resume and providing an immediate action plan."*

---

### [1:35 – 1:55] 4-Week Actionable Roadmap
- **Visual**: Switch to `"4-Week Roadmap & Project"` tab. Scroll through Weeks 1 to 4.
- **Voiceover**:  
  *"HireReady AI doesn't just tell students what they lack; it teaches them how to fix it. Our Planner Agent generated a 4-week structured curriculum. Week 1 covers containerization fundamentals with Docker; Week 2 focuses on AWS S3 and Lambda deployment; Week 3 tackles advanced DSA; and Week 4 covers mock interview prep."*

---

### [1:55 – 2:20] The Recommended Capstone Project
- **Visual**: Highlight the Recommended Placement Project card.
- **Voiceover**:  
  *"To make Aarav stand out to recruiters, the copilot recommends one targeted capstone project: a 'Distributed Rate-Limited Task Queue'. It explicitly bridges his missing AWS and Docker gaps, outlines the exact tech stack, and explains why this specific project will shine on his resume during technical rounds."*

---

### [2:20 – 2:45] Interactive Mock Interview Practice
- **Visual**: Click `"Practice Interview"`. Show Question 1 (DSA / Technical). Type a response and click `"Submit Answer"`. Show scored feedback (/10), strengths, areas for improvement, and the model exemplar answer.
- **Voiceover**:  
  *"Finally, students can test their readiness in an AI mock interview round. The Interview Coach Agent serves 5 customized questions spanning technical, DSA, project, and behavioral categories. When Aarav submits his answer, he receives an instant evaluation out of 10, bulleted strengths, improvement tips, and a model answer to study."*

---

### [2:45 – 3:00] AWS Architecture & Conclusion
- **Visual**: Show architecture diagram and GitHub repo with 46 passing unit tests.
- **Voiceover**:  
  *"HireReady AI is built 100% serverless on AWS: FastAPI on AWS Lambda, Amazon API Gateway, Amazon Bedrock running Nova Lite via the Strands Agents SDK, and AWS Amplify Hosting. 46 passing unit tests ensure deterministic reliability. HireReady AI empowers every student to become internship-ready. Thank you!"*
